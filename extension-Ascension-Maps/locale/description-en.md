# **Ascension Maps - Stronghold Crusader**

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Krarilotus/Ascension/ucp3-ascension/extension-Ascension-Multiplayer/locale/ascension-multiplayer-match.png)

Ascension Maps is a curated collection of **114 skirmish maps** for Ascension Multiplayer, covering competitive duels, team battles, free-for-all matches, and battles against AI opponents.

## **Included maps**

- **1v1 maps — 54**: Competitive maps designed for direct duels between two players.
- **Team maps — 39**: Maps for 2v2, 2v3, 3v3, 4v4, and 2v2v2v2 battles.
- **Free-for-all and player-count maps — 14**: Open formats for four to eight players.
- **Versus-AI maps — 7**: Scenarios intended for players fighting AI opponents.

## **Map creators**

Maps were created by members of the **Ascension Group**, including **Altaruss'28, CIO61, Lord Hühnerfutter, F2erni8, Krarilotus, Crusader Pilaw, Heroesflorian**, and more.
