# **Ascension Multiplayer Balance Mod - Stronghold Crusader**  

**Ascension Multiplayer Balance Mod** redefines the multiplayer experience of Stronghold Crusader, transforming it into a competitive, fast-paced RTS-style game. Built on the **Unofficial Crusader Patch**, it introduces enhanced gameplay mechanics, balanced starting conditions, bug fixes, and a finely-tuned economy system for dynamic multiplayer action.  

## Automarket update (1.0.10)

Requires Automarket **1.1.0 or newer within 1.x**, including multiplayer synchronization and trading fee fixes. All participants must use the same Automarket version. For saves made with Automarket 1.0.0, each player must confirm **Save & Close** in Automarket before automated trading resumes.

---

## **Core Features**  

### **1. Customized Starting Conditions**  
- **Starting Resources**: 100 wood, 10 stone, 30 bread, and 10 of each other food type.  
- **Starting Army**: 2 Arabian Archers, 2 Slingers, and 1 Arabian Swordsman.  
- **Balanced Economy Start**: No free gold, encouraging immediate economic development and tactical decisions from the first minute.

---

### **2. Dynamic Economy & Combat**  
- **Progressive Economy**: Manage farms, mines, and industries while balancing offensive and defensive needs.  
- **Tactical Combat**: Refined unit stats and combat mechanics ensure that army composition, positioning, and map control matter more than ever.  
- **Flexible Playstyles**: Develop your strategies—whether by rushing, defending, or focusing on long-term expansion.  

---

### **3. Competitive Map Pack & AI Support**  
- **50+ Custom Multiplayer Maps**: Balanced for fair play and tactical depth, with a variety of environments supporting diverse playstyles.  
- **Ascension AIs**: Unique AIs are tuned for maximum efficiency. Beware: **Rat AI** is uniquely built for the Crossroads map as an AFK bounty AI.
